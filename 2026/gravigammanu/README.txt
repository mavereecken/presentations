Multi-Messenger Astronomy with LIGO-Virgo-KAGRA — offline slides
=================================================================

PRESENT
  Open index.html in Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari (double-click works, no internet needed).
  Press F for fullscreen, ? for all shortcuts.

  →/Space/PgDn next · ←/PgUp previous · 12 + Enter jump to slide 12
  S speaker window (notes, next slide, timer) · N notes on screen
  G slide list by section · B black screen · P play/pause videos

  Videos start when their slide opens (the GW-sky video on slide 4 plays WITH sound).
  Click a video to pause/play. Links open in a new tab.
  Presentation clickers that send PgUp/PgDn or arrow keys work out of the box.

PDF
  Print the page (Ctrl/Cmd+P) → Save as PDF: one slide per page (videos show their still frame).

HOST ONLINE
  Upload the whole folder as-is to any static host (GitHub Pages, Netlify, your institute's web space).
  Keep index.html, fonts.css, fonts/ and assets/ together.

Animated GIFs are stored as small looping MP4s, and large images are resized WebP copies, to keep the folder small.

Fonts: IBM Plex Sans and Space Grotesk (SIL Open Font License), included in fonts/.
Exported from the Claude slides deck on 29 Sept 2026; later edits in the deck are not included.
